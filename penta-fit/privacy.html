Penta-Fit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

EM Studios ("we") makes the mobile game Penta-Fit for Android and iOS.

This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Penta-Fit saves your game in progress, best score, number of board clears, chosen block style, and sound, voice and vibration settings on your device, so you can continue where you left off.

This information stays on your device.

We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Penta-Fit has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

Penta-Fit offers optional rewarded video ads: when you run out of room, you can choose to watch an ad to continue playing, and you can watch one to get an extra power-up.

Between some games, Penta-Fit also shows a full-screen ad.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played, improve it and decide when ads appear, Penta-Fit uses Google Analytics for Firebase.

It records how you play: when games start and end, scores, levels, how long games last, continues, power-ups used and ads shown.

Firebase also collects standard app information, such as an app instance identifier, your device model and operating system, the app version, your country and approximate location derived from your IP address, and when you open the game.

Where available and allowed, it may also use your device's advertising identifier.

This information is not linked to your name or contact details, and Google keeps it for at most 14 months.

See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Penta-Fit asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

Analytics follows the same choices: nothing is collected until your choices allow it.

You can change your choice at any time from Settings, then Privacy choices, in the game.

On iPhone and iPad, Penta-Fit asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier, and you can change this later in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Rewarded ads are always optional, and the game can be played without them.

Children

Penta-Fit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
